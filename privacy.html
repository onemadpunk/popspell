Privacy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version.

  • PopSpell is free. There are no adverts, no tracking and no selling of anyone's information.
  • Children don't make accounts. They type a first name or a code their teacher gives them.
  • Practice results are stored so the child's teacher can see progress and plan lessons. Only that teacher (and PopSpell's site administrators) can see them.
  • Results and pupil records are stored in London by Google Firebase, which works for us under a data processing agreement. Teachers' sign-in, the website itself and its fonts are handled by Google and GitHub, which may process them in the United States (see where it's stored).
  • If a teacher connects Google Calendar, PopSpell only adds, moves and removes its own lesson events. It never reads the rest of the calendar.

For children

  • PopSpell asks for your first name so it can save your stars and sounds. You don't need to type your full name.
  • If you've joined a class, your teacher can see what you practise and how you get on, even while you're doing it. PopSpell tells you this under your name with a 👀.
  • Only your teacher and the people who run PopSpell can see your work. Your teacher might also make a page about your progress for your grown-ups at home.
  • There are no adverts or chat, and strangers can't contact you.
  • If you're worried about anything, or want something taken away, tell a parent, carer or teacher. You can also email us yourself.

Who we are

If your child's tutor isn't Sara Green, that tutor decides how your child's information is used and is its controller. PopSpell stores it for them under a written agreement. Please contact the tutor first; we'll help them answer you.

What we collect, and why

Children's devices sign in to Google Firebase anonymously, with no name or email. A child can never see other children's results or the class list.

What teachers record about their pupils

In the teacher area, a teacher can record about each pupil: a parent's email address and whether they asked for lesson reminders; the next lesson's date and time; homework; a year group; notes on things to bear in mind (for example a learning difficulty or an additional need); what the child enjoys; a note for home; and the dates of parents' night reports. Only that teacher and PopSpell's site administrators can see this.

Notes about a child's needs can include health information, which the law treats as more sensitive. We only record this with a parent's agreement, and only what helps us teach the child.

Parents

A parent's email address is used only for messages from their child's teacher, such as lesson reminders and home login links. PopSpell doesn't send email itself: the teacher sends these from their own email account. If the teacher makes a parent page, a summary of the child's progress and the teacher's note is kept at a long private link that can't be guessed. Anyone given the link can open that one page, so please don't share it. The teacher can turn the link off at any time.

Teachers

Teachers sign in with Google. We keep their name, email address and role so they can see their own classes, and the classes they create.

Google Calendar

Teachers can choose to put their lessons into their own Google Calendar. If they press Sync to my Google Calendar, Google asks them to allow PopSpell to see and edit events on their calendars (the calendar.events permission). PopSpell uses that permission only to:

  • add one event per pupil for their next lesson, with the pupil's name, the class name, the homework set and a link back to the teacher area;
  • move that event when the lesson date or time changes in PopSpell; and
  • remove that event when the lesson date is cleared.

PopSpell never reads, copies or changes any other events, and never shares calendar information with anyone. Google's permission is kept only in the teacher's open browser tab, for about an hour. It's never stored on PopSpell's servers. Teachers can remove the permission at any time in their Google Account, under Security → Your connections to third-party apps and services.

PopSpell's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Our legal basis

  • Legitimate interests: we keep children's practice results, lesson dates and homework because it's needed to teach them and track their progress, which their parent has asked us to do. We have weighed this against the child's interests and keep it to what helps their learning. You can object at any time.
  • Consent: lesson reminder emails and parent pages are only used when a parent agrees, and a parent can change their mind at any time.
  • Explicit consent: notes about a child's needs are only recorded with the parent's explicit agreement.
  • Teachers choose whether to connect their own Google Calendar, and can disconnect it at any time.

Where it's stored, and who else handles it

  • Google Firebase (Google Cloud) stores results and teacher records in its London (europe-west2) data centre, under Google's data processing terms. It also handles sign-in: teachers' names and email addresses, and the anonymous sign-in on children's devices, which Google may process in the United States.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website itself. Like any web host, it sees visitors' IP addresses when it sends the pages.
  • Google Fonts provides the website's fonts, so a visitor's browser fetches them from Google.
  • The code that connects PopSpell to Firebase is also fetched from Google's servers (gstatic.com) when a page opens.

GitHub and Google are based in the United States. Information sent there is covered by the UK's data bridge with the US (the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Notes about a child's needs stay in London.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share anyone's information.

On the device

PopSpell keeps some information in the browser's own storage so it works offline and remembers settings: the child's name or code, their class, their saved progress, reading speed and other settings. Results are kept on the device until they reach the server. None of this is used to track anyone. Clearing the browser's site data removes it, and on a child's device Not me? (beside their name) forgets the name.

On a teacher's computer, the browser also keeps a copy of their classes so the teacher area works with a poor connection. Signing out removes that copy (with any other PopSpell tabs closed first), so teachers should always sign out on a shared computer. Google Calendar permission is kept only in the open tab and goes when it closes.

PopSpell doesn't set cookies (Google's own sign-in window may, when a teacher signs in with Google). Everything it keeps in the browser is needed for something the person has asked for (practising, saving progress, signing in or their own settings), so there's no cookie banner.

How long we keep it

Results and pupil records are kept while the child has lessons, and deleted within 12 months of their last lesson, or sooner if a parent asks. Parent pages are kept until the teacher turns them off, and are deleted with the rest of the child's records.

Your rights

Parents, on their child's behalf, and teachers can ask to see the information we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it's used. Email onemadpunk@gmail.com and we'll reply within one month. Children can ask too: in Scotland, children aged 12 or over are usually able to make these requests themselves. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

PopSpell is made for children and young people from early years to age 16, who use it with a teacher or a parent. We collect as little as possible about them, show nothing publicly, and have no chat, adverts or contact with strangers.
