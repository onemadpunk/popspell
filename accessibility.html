Accessibility

Last reviewed: 31 October 2026

PopSpell is for every child, including children with dyslexia, other additional needs or a disability. We want everyone to be able to use it, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This page covers popspell.com: the pupil lists and activities, the teacher area, parent pages, help and privacy pages.

What you can do

  • Hear every word, sentence and question read aloud, by a recorded voice or the device's own voice, and change how fast it speaks (teachers: ⚙️ Settings).
  • Use PopSpell with a keyboard: every button, list and game can be reached with Tab and pressed with Enter or Space. The sound-mapping games can be played by pressing buttons as well as by dragging.
  • Zoom in to 400%, or use a phone, without text being cut off or needing to scroll sideways (a few wide tables scroll in their own box).
  • Turn off animation: PopSpell follows the "reduce motion" setting on your device.
  • Read in a clear, child-friendly font (Sassoon if your device has it, otherwise Andika).
  • Choose the time for the times tables check practice: 6 seconds a question like the real check, double time, or no time limit. Nothing else in PopSpell is timed.
  • Have as many tries as you need: teachers can set 1 to 5 tries for each word, and a word that is missed comes back later for more practice.

What we know isn't right yet

  • Listening activities (such as hearing which sounds are in a word) are about hearing, so a child who is deaf or hard of hearing will need an adult to say the words or sign them.
  • In the teacher area, the row of tabs works with Tab and Enter, but not with the arrow keys that screen reader users may expect.
  • PopSpell has a light colour scheme only. It doesn't change when a device is set to dark mode.
  • Printed worksheets and reports are designed for paper and aren't tagged for screen readers.
  • The site's fonts come from Google Fonts. If they're blocked, PopSpell uses your device's own fonts instead.

How we checked

PopSpell was last checked on 31 October 2026. We tested the pages with a keyboard, with automated checks for colour contrast, labels and headings, and at a screen width of 320 pixels. This was our own review, not an independent audit. We fix problems as we find them.

Tell us about a problem

If something in PopSpell is hard to use, or you need something in a different format, please tell us. Email onemadpunk+popspell@gmail.com and say which page and what happened. We'll reply as soon as we can.
